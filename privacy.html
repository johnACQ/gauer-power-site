Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Gauer Power Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. It applies to gauerpower.ca and any quote or contact form we run.

Information we collect

When you contact us or request a quote, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, town or service location, and details about the electrical work you need. We collect this only when you give it to us directly.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and give you a quote
  • To schedule, confirm, and follow up on appointments and service work
  • To contact you by phone, email, or text message about your job
  • To send service reminders and request feedback after a job

SMS / text message communications

Gauer Power Ltd. collects phone numbers through our website forms and by direct verbal consent. With your consent, your phone number may be used to contact you via SMS text message for:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Quote follow-ups and service updates
  • Review and feedback requests after a completed job

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help.

We will never sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS consent with any third party for marketing purposes. SMS consent is not shared with any affiliate, partner, or third-party service provider.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers that help us run our business (for example, our CRM and scheduling software) strictly so they can perform services for us. They are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal or accounting requirements, then securely delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or stop contacting you, at any time. Reply STOP to any text to opt out of SMS, or contact us using the details below.
